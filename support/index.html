Slide Capture — Support

Slide Capture photographs presentation slides from your seat, straightens them and keeps them together by talk.

Getting started

Tap + to start a talk and give it a title. Point the camera at the screen: a green outline appears around the slide and follows it. Press the shutter, check the corners in the editor, and tap Save. The camera comes back for the next slide. Tap Done when the talk is over.

Frequently asked questions

The outline is on the wrong thing, or there is no outline.

Tap the slide in the camera view. A yellow square shows where you tapped; the camera focuses and sets the exposure there, and the app looks for the slide around that point. Tap Auto to go back to automatic. You can also just snap: the corners can be set by hand in the editor.

The slide is too bright or washed out in the camera.

Drag the brightness slider (sun icon) to the left before you snap, or tap the slide so the exposure is measured there. Projectors are much brighter than the room around them.

I am sitting far from the screen.

Use the zoom slider. On iPhones with a telephoto camera the app switches to it automatically as you zoom in.

The corners are not exactly on the slide.

In the editor, drag any corner; a magnified view helps you place it. Pinch or double-tap to zoom in, or use the four arrow buttons to jump to a corner. Snap to edge moves the corners onto the nearest border, Found border goes back to what the app detected, and Full photo keeps the whole picture.

The slide came out sideways.

Tap Rotate in the editor until it is upright.

The colours look too strong, or I want the photo as it was.

Tap Enhanced in the editor to switch the enhancement off for that slide; the button then reads Original. Tap it again to switch it back on. The slider above the buttons makes the slide lighter or darker.

Can I use photos I already took?

Yes. Open a talk and tap the import button (bottom left) to pick a photo from your library. It opens in the same editor.

How do I export a talk?

Open the talk and tap the share button (top right): Export PDF makes one PDF with a slide per page, Share as photos shares the images, and Save to Photos adds them to your photo library. Touch and hold a single slide to share or delete just that one.

How do I rename or delete a talk?

Rename it from the share menu inside the talk. To delete it, swipe left on the talk in the list.

Where are my slides stored?

On your device only, inside the app. There is no account and no cloud copy, so export anything you want to keep before deleting the app.

Is it all right to photograph slides?

That depends on the event and the speaker. Only photograph presentations where it is allowed.
