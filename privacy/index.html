Privacy Policy — Slide Capture

Effective 10 November 2026

Slide Capture (“the app”) is an iPhone and iPad app that photographs presentation slides, straightens them and keeps them together by talk. This policy explains what the app does with data.

The short version

The app collects nothing. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your slides stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Data the app processes on your device

  • Camera. While the camera screen is open, the app looks at the camera image to find the slide and draw its outline. Nothing is recorded until you press the shutter. The photo you take is straightened and enhanced on the device.
  • Your slides. Saved slides and their thumbnails are stored in the app's own folder on the device, grouped by talk, with the time each was captured. Deleting a slide or a talk in the app deletes its files. Deleting the app deletes everything.
  • Imported photos. If you import a photo, you pick it with the system photo picker; the app receives only the photo you chose and has no access to the rest of your library.
  • Settings. App preferences are kept on the device with iOS user defaults.

Data you export

Export PDF and Share as photos hand files to the iOS share sheet. Where they go — Files, AirDrop, Mail, another app — is your choice. Save to Photos adds the slides to your photo library; for that the app asks for permission to add photos only, and it cannot read your library.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not use third-party SDKs, analytics or crash reporting.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.
  • It does not record audio or video.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Other people's content

Slides you photograph may belong to the presenter. Only photograph presentations where that is allowed, and respect the presenter's rights when you share them.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app does with data, this policy will be updated and the change noted in the App Store release notes.
